This paper presents a two-stage pulmonary nodule detection pipeline for lung CT analysis. The method combines U-Net++ for high-recall candidate generation with Multi-Scale Gradual Integration CNN for false-positive reduction.

On LUNA16-oriented evaluation, nodule-level recall improved from 93.4% to 97.8%, and classification AUC improved from 0.852 to 0.901.
