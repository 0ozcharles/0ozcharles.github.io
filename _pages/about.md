I am Zhaoxu Zheng, a Computer Science undergraduate at Harbin University of Science and Technology. My academic interests center on computer vision, deep learning, and medical image analysis, especially lung CT analysis and automated pulmonary nodule detection.

My current research work explores two-stage medical image analysis pipelines: high-recall candidate generation followed by false-positive reduction with 3D deep learning models. I care about reproducible experiments, quantitative evaluation, and building systems that can be inspected carefully rather than treated as black boxes.

I am looking for research opportunities where I can continue developing as a researcher in computer vision and medical AI. I am especially interested in labs that combine strong machine learning methodology with clinically meaningful evaluation and careful analysis of failure cases.

## Research Interests

- Computer vision
- Deep learning
- Medical image analysis
